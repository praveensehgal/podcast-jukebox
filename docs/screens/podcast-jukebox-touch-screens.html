Podcast Jukebox · touch screens · for approval

Tap to choose. Press to play.

Everything you choose, you tap: a channel, an episode, a setting, the volume. The three buttons on top only do play, pause and stop, so they work without looking. No swiping anywhere: the touch layer on this board needs a firm press, so every list pages with big ▲ ▼ arrows instead.

Tap targets are labelled with their size in millimetres on the real glass. Real size assumes a 96 dpi monitor.

Rules the screens follow

  • Finger sizeNothing smaller than 10 mm tall. Rows are 68 px (10.4 mm), arrows 72 × 112 px (11 × 17 mm), now-playing buttons 13 mm. Keyboard keys are the one exception at 7 mm, used once at setup.
  • No swipingResistive touch reads a firm press well and a drag badly. Lists move a page at a time with ▲ ▼, and the page count is always visible.
  • One tap does one thingTap a channel: its episodes. Tap an episode: it plays. No highlight-then-confirm step.
  • Back is top-leftEvery screen below the channel list has ‹ in the same corner. STOP on top also takes him home from anywhere.
  • Buttons on topPLAY resumes the last episode from any screen. PAUSE toggles. STOP stops, saves the place, and returns to the channel list.
  • Where am IWhile something plays, every screen shows a green ♪ pill in the header; tapping it opens Now Playing.
  • Big typeLists 23 px, titles 19 px, nothing under 14 px. Same Montserrat that ships with LVGL.

What changes in the firmware

  • Touch onXPT2046 enabled and calibrated once with the four-corner tool from your macro pad.
  • Knob code outScroll and volume knobs removed; the input stream becomes taps plus the three top buttons.
  • ScreensAll eight rebuilt to these layouts. The data, audio, resolver and update code stay as they are.
  • KeyboardReplaces the knob character strip for Wi-Fi passwords. The phone QR route stays.
  • ButtonsThree GPIOs for play, pause and stop, debounced, with a hold on STOP for Settings as a backup.
