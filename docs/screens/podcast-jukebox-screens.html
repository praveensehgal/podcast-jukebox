Podcast Jukebox · UI mockup · 480 × 320

Three screens, two knobs, three buttons

Rendered at the panel's native pixel size in Montserrat, the typeface LVGL ships on the ESP32, so what you see is what the Sunton 3.5-inch display will draw. Use the size switch to judge legibility: the panel is physically 73 × 49 mm, so at 1:1 your monitor shows it larger than life.

Real size assumes a 96 dpi monitor. Hold a ruler to the bezel: it should read about 73 mm wide.

Design rules
  • Type sizesLists 24 px, episode titles 19 px, Now Playing title 23 px, time 24 px. Smallest anything on screen: 14 px metadata. On this panel 24 px is about 3.7 mm tall, the size of newspaper headline text at arm's length.
  • Rows per screen8 channels, 4 episodes. Never fewer; a shorter list feels like something is missing.
  • HighlightWhole row inverts to #b5431b with white text. One highlight, always visible, never a thin outline.
  • One rule for OKOK opens whatever is highlighted. Channel list to episode list to playing. Pushing the scroll knob is the same as OK.
  • StopStops audio, saves the position, returns to the channel list. It is also the only "back" he needs.
  • ResumeEvery episode remembers where he stopped. OK on a partly played episode picks up there. The thin bar under the row shows how far he got.
  • Pause anywherePause from any screen resumes the last episode, so he can wander the lists while listening.
  • No touchThe panel has a touch layer. It is disabled. Knobs and buttons only, so nothing happens from a stray hand.
  • VolumeOverlay slides up on the first click of the knob and hides 2 s after the last. It never covers the title.
  • SettingsLive past the last channel and behind a 3 s hold on STOP. Same rows, same knob, same OK. Nothing in Settings stops the audio.
  • Wi-FiQR route first: phone joins the box's hotspot, picks the network, types the password. On-box character entry is the fallback, meant for the installer.
  • BluetoothOutput only, to headphones or hearing aids. Paired headphones reconnect on power-up and the speaker mutes automatically. Nothing to do on the box after the first pairing.
