# 001 - Two knobs, three buttons, no touch

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Status:** accepted. The two knobs and the OK button were replaced by touch on 2026-10-10 (decision 004); PAUSE and STOP, and what STOP means, stand.
**Context:** The device is for a 60-year-old who watches TV all day and will not use an app. The first proposal was a touch-tile remote; Praveen replaced it with a car-radio grammar.
**Decision:** SCROLL encoder (push = OK), VOLUME pot, and three buttons OK / PAUSE / STOP. OK opens whatever is highlighted (channel → episodes → play). STOP stops, saves the position and returns to the channel list, and is the only "back". PAUSE toggles, and resumes the last episode from any screen. The panel's touch layer is disabled.
**Alternatives:** Touch tiles (rejected: stray touches, no tactile feedback). A fourth "back" button (rejected: STOP already covers it).
**Consequences:** Every screen must be navigable with one rotary and one confirm. Settings live past the last channel and behind a 3 s STOP hold. Password entry is a rotary character strip, with the QR captive portal as the primary route.
