Before I cut the STL, four caliper numbers make the fit exact. If you would rather print the look first, I cut it from the nominal values above with an extra millimetre of clearance.
  1. The display glass: its width and height, and the gap from the left edge of the board to the left edge of the glass.
  2. Total thickness from the front of the glass to the tallest part on the back of the board.
  3. The speaker: outer diameter, and depth from the front of its frame to the back of the magnet.
  4. The tact switches for the three buttons: total height from the pins' base to the top of the stem.
