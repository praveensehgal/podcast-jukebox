A touch wedge the size of the board

The sloped face is the screen with a thin bezel and nothing else, like the printer display on your desk. Every control lives on the glass: big rows to tap, a bar along the bottom for PAUSE, STOP and volume. The speaker fires out of the tall vertical back. Footprint is a hand's width.
